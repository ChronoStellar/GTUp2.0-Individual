## ✨ Features

GTUp! is a symphony of innovative features, engineered to cater to professionals, students, and creators worldwide. Here’s what makes it extraordinary:

### Core Features 🛠️
- **Customizable Work & Break Timers** ⏱️  
  Tailor your productivity and break intervals with precision to align with your unique rhythm.
- **Microbreak Reminders** 🔔  
  Intelligent prompts to take microbreaks during work sessions, designed to boost cognitive performance and reduce fatigue.
- **Live Activity Display** 📊  
  Stay informed with real-time updates on remaining time, elegantly showcased through a dynamic interface.
- **WatchOS Integration** ⌚  
  Seamless notifications on your Apple Watch for effortless connectivity to your productivity flow.

### Additional Features 🎉
- **Standby Mode** 🌙  
  Optimizes resource usage during idle periods while maintaining accessibility.
- **Data Summary Dashboard** 📈  
  Dive into visually stunning insights on your work focus and break patterns to refine your strategy.
- **Profile Customization** 🧑‍💼  
  Personalize your experience with a bespoke profile that reflects your identity.

## 🖥️ User Experience

GTUp! offers a gesture-driven interface that’s as intuitive as it is elegant:
- **Start & Stop Timer** ▶️: Tap *Start Work* to begin; long-press to pause gracefully.
- **Timer Setup** ⚙️: Swipe right to configure work and break intervals effortlessly.
- **Data Insights** 📉: Swipe left to explore your productivity metrics.
- **Profile Customization** 🎨: Tap the profile icon to curate your unique experience.
- **Break Transition** 🌿: Seamlessly shifts to break mode with a notification when work time ends.
